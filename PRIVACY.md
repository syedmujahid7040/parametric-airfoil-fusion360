# Privacy Policy – Parametric Airfoil by Syed Mujahid

_Last updated: 16 October 2026_

Parametric Airfoil is a free add-in for Autodesk Fusion, published by Syed Mujahid.

## Data we collect

**None.** The add-in does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data, usage data, analytics or design data.

- It does not connect to the internet or to any server.
- It does not use cookies, tracking, telemetry or crash reporting.
- It reads only the airfoil `.dat` file that **you** choose in the dialog, and only to draw the sketch in your open Fusion design. The file stays on your computer.
- Everything it creates (sketches, dimensions and user parameters) is saved in your Fusion design, under your control, like any other Fusion feature.

## Third parties

The add-in shares no data with any third party. Your use of Autodesk Fusion itself, and of the Autodesk Design and Make Marketplace, is covered by Autodesk's own privacy statement.

## Retention and deletion

Because no data is collected, none is retained. To remove everything the add-in created, delete the sketch and the user parameters from your design. To remove the add-in, stop and uninstall it in **UTILITIES › ADD-INS › Scripts and Add-Ins**, or from the Marketplace's app manager.

## Consent and contact

There is no data collection to consent to or withdraw from. For questions about this policy, open an issue at
https://github.com/syedmujahid7040/parametric-airfoil-fusion360/issues

## Changes

If a future version ever changes what data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be noted in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).
